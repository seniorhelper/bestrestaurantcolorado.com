Road-trip dining in Colorado

Five drives from Denver, the recognized restaurants you pass on each, and the practical advice that keeps a dinner reservation from colliding with a mountain pass.

Updated 10 October 2026  /  By Zach Wennstedt  /  Editorial picks, criteria explained below

Plan the drive around the dinner

Colorado is big, mountainous and unevenly fed. Most of its recognized restaurants cluster on the Front Range and in a handful of resort towns, with long stretches of highway in between. A good food road trip here works backwards: pick the one or two dinners that matter, then build the route and the overnight stops around them, leaving slack for weather and traffic.

Below are five drives from Denver, each listing the MICHELIN Guide and James Beard-recognized restaurants along the way, in the order you would reach them. We do not list hours or prices, and conditions on mountain roads change quickly, so check the road and the restaurant before you set off.

Before any mountain drive. Check COtrip.org from the Colorado Department of Transportation for closures, travel times and traction-law alerts. In winter, Colorado can require adequate tires or chains on I-70 and mountain highways when its traction law is in effect. Carry water, layers and a charged phone, and avoid planning a tight dinner reservation on a day you are crossing a pass in a storm.

Five drives from Denver

Route 1  /  I-70 west to the Grand Valley

Denver to Grand Junction

The classic cross-mountain drive: up through the Eisenhower-Johnson Memorial Tunnels into Summit County, over Vail Pass, through Glenwood Canyon and down into the warm, low Grand Valley. In good conditions it takes about four hours without stops; as a food trip it deserves two or three days.

  1. Breckenridge (a short detour south from I-70 at Frisco, 9,600 ft): Rootstalk and Radicato, both MICHELIN Recommended since 2026.
  2. Vail: Sweet Basil and Osaki's, listed by MICHELIN every year since 2023.
  3. Beaver Creek and Avon: the formal Splendido at the Chateau up the hill and Mirabelle's farmhouse below.
  4. Glenwood Springs: nothing recognized yet; turn south here for Aspen.
  5. Palisade: orchards, farm stands and wineries rather than award-listed restaurants. Best from July to September.
  6. Grand Junction, the low point of the trip: Bin 707 Foodbar, where Josh Niernberg was shortlisted nationally by the James Beard Foundation in 2026.

More: Vail and Breckenridge guide, Western Slope guide.

Route 2  /  to Aspen

Denver to Aspen, by season

In winter the road route is I-70 to Glenwood Springs and then Highway 82 up the Roaring Fork Valley. In summer, Highway 82 over Independence Pass is shorter and spectacular, but it closes for the winter and is narrow and winding, so check its status on COtrip before you rely on it.

  1. Aspen: four MICHELIN listings, led by the starred Bosq, with Element 47, Prospect and Mawa's Kitchen behind it.

More: Aspen restaurants guide.

Route 3  /  I-25 south

Denver to Colorado Springs and Pueblo

The easiest drive on this page: interstate the whole way, no high passes, and about an hour from Denver to Colorado Springs in light traffic, with Pueblo roughly an hour further on.

  1. Parker, a short detour east of I-25: Poulette Bakeshop, on the 2026 James Beard long list for bakeries nationwide.
  2. Colorado Springs: two first-time MICHELIN listings, Four by Brother Luck downtown and The Penrose Room at The Broadmoor, each with a 2026 special award.
  3. Pueblo: no award-listed restaurants, but the heart of chile country from August to October.

More: Colorado Springs guide.

Route 4  /  north along the foothills

Denver to Boulder and Lyons

A half-day loop, or a gentle start to a trip toward Rocky Mountain National Park.

  1. Edgewater, hugging Denver's west edge: Gladys, whose two chefs made the 2026 regional James Beard long list.
  2. Boulder: eleven listings, headed by the starred Frasca Food and Wine, the state's only Outstanding Restaurant winner (2025).
  3. Lyons: Marigold, Theo Adley's restaurant at the mouth of the St. Vrain canyon.
  4. Estes Park: gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park, with no award-listed restaurants so far.

More: Front Range guide.

Route 5  /  the long way southwest

Denver to Telluride

A full day's drive across several mountain passes, best split with an overnight. Many visitors fly instead, into Montrose or Telluride's own airport. Telluride rewards the effort with The National, the San Juans' first MICHELIN listing, and Van Atta, a bar the James Beard Foundation long-listed in its first year.

More: Telluride and Western Slope guide.

Timing, altitude and reservations on the road

Seasons

Winter, from about Thanksgiving to early April, fills mountain dining rooms and makes the roads least predictable. The summer months are easier going and bring local produce onto menus. In the shoulder weeks of spring and late fall, some mountain kitchens close for a short break, so a road trip in late April or early November needs more checking ahead.

Altitude

Many routes climb from Denver's 5,280 feet past 10,000 feet at the passes and tunnels. Visitors from low elevations can feel it: headaches, poor sleep and a sharper effect from alcohol are common on the first day. Hydrate, keep the first evening simple, and hold the big tasting menu until you have acclimatised.

Reservations

Book the anchor dinners first, as soon as each restaurant's booking window opens, then plan the drive. For counter and tasting-menu restaurants, which seat few people a night, cancel promptly if road conditions mean you cannot make it: a no-show hurts a small restaurant badly.

Where the register thins out

Large parts of Colorado have no MICHELIN or James Beard-recognized restaurants at all, among them Steamboat, Crested Butte, Durango, Salida and Fort Collins, even after the 2026 selection. That does not mean the food is poor, only that neither body has named a restaurant there yet. Our statewide register shows every recognized restaurant by elevation, which makes it easy to see what is near your route.

Questions

Common questions

How do I check road conditions before a Colorado road trip?

Use COtrip.org from the Colorado Department of Transportation, which shows closures, travel times, cameras and traction-law alerts for I-70 and the mountain highways.

Is Independence Pass open year-round?

No. Highway 82 over Independence Pass between Twin Lakes and Aspen closes for the winter. In winter, drive to Aspen via I-70 and Glenwood Springs.

Which recognized restaurants are along I-70?

Rootstalk and Radicato in Breckenridge (a short detour), Sweet Basil and Osaki's in Vail, Splendido at the Chateau in Beaver Creek, Mirabelle in Avon, and Bin 707 Foodbar in Grand Junction at the western end.

How should I handle altitude on a food road trip?

Drink more water than usual, eat and drink lightly on the first day at elevation, and save the longest tasting menu with wine for later in the trip, once you have adjusted.
