Telluride and the Western Slope

The San Juans got their first MICHELIN listing in 2026, and the Grand Valley has one of Colorado's most decorated chefs. Here is the short, verified list, and the harvest calendar that goes with it.

The far side of the Divide

West of the Continental Divide, Colorado's restaurant map thins out and its farm map fills in. The San Juan Mountains in the southwest are about as remote as Colorado dining gets, and the Grand Valley around Grand Junction and Palisade is the warmest and lowest, the only part of Colorado where peaches and wine grapes are a major crop. Neither region was inside the MICHELIN Guide's boundary before 2026.

The first statewide selection added one restaurant out here: The National in Telluride. Inspectors could also consider Grand Junction, Durango, Ouray and Silverton, and listed nothing in any of them. That leaves a short list, which is useful in its own way. Every name below has been independently recognized, and between them they are reason enough to plan a western Colorado trip around dinner.

How these picks are ordered. We list the MICHELIN entry first, then the two James Beard-recognized restaurants by region, Telluride before the Grand Valley. We do not grade kitchens, and a place on this list cannot be purchased.

  1. The National

    MICHELIN Recommended (new 2026) · 100 E. Colorado Ave., Telluride

    In September 2026 this became the San Juans' debut MICHELIN entry. Executive chef Chris Thompson's menu is described in listings as Mediterranean-leaning, with shared plates, fish and pasta. The National has also hosted collaboration dinners with other Colorado chefs, including Bosq's Barclay Dodge from Aspen. We could not verify its own website, so we link to the MICHELIN Guide; check current details there or by contacting the restaurant.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

  2. Van Atta

    2026 James Beard semifinalist, Best New Bar · Telluride

    Telluride's second recognized address came via the James Beard Foundation, which named it on the 2026 long list for the national new-bar category. Confirm hours and what food is served before you plan dinner around it.

    James Beard Awards

  3. Bin 707 Foodbar

    2026 James Beard nominee, Outstanding Chef · Grand Junction

    Josh Niernberg, who owns and cooks at Bin 707, reached the national shortlist for the James Beard Outstanding Chef award in 2026, his second straight year at that stage; Michael Tusk of San Francisco's Quince took the medal. With MICHELIN listing nothing in Grand Junction in its first eligible year, which leaves Bin 707 as the Western Slope's clearest claim to national attention.

    James Beard Awards

Telluride: a box canyon at 8,750 feet

Telluride sits at the end of a box canyon ringed by peaks above 13,000 feet. Its isolation is part of its appeal and the root of its hardest problem: housing for restaurant workers. Rental vacancy has been reported at around one percent, and there have been summer days when restaurants closed simply because they could not staff a shift. Hours and service days can change at short notice, so confirm directly before you go.

Most visitors arrive by road or through the regional airports at Telluride and Montrose. Like every mountain town, Telluride has quiet shoulder seasons in spring and late fall when some kitchens close for a break. Summer festivals and the ski season are the busy periods, and both reward booking early.

Durango, Ouray and Silverton

None of these towns has a register entry after 2026. Durango, at 6,512 feet, has a year-round population and a cuisine shaped by Southwestern ingredients rather than resort convention, and it was among the towns coverage of the MICHELIN expansion flagged as one to watch. It did not appear on the 2026 list. Our register only follows the two award bodies, so if you are in the area, use local knowledge and recent reporting, and watch for future MICHELIN and James Beard announcements.

The Grand Valley: orchards, vineyards and Bin 707

Grand Junction sits lower than any other town on our register, under 4,600 feet. Just upriver, Palisade's orchards turn a strip of high desert into peach country thanks to irrigation from the Colorado River, and from summer into early autumn the fruit stands line the roads.

Wine grows here too, more than anywhere else in the state. The Grand Valley American Viticultural Area surrounds Palisade, and the West Elks AVA lies to the southeast around Paonia; the state's wine industry board at coloradowine.org lists wineries and tasting rooms. Restaurants here can pour wine made a few miles away, something no ski-town kitchen supplied over the passes can match.

Festivals worth planning around

  • Palisade Peach Festival. Held each August at Riverbend Park in Palisade. Parking in town is limited and shuttles have run from Grand Junction; check the Palisade Chamber of Commerce for next year's dates.
  • Olathe Sweet Corn Festival. Held each August, celebrating the sweet corn grown around Olathe near Montrose. Organizers post details on their own channels each summer.
  • Harvest season in general. Late summer is when Western Slope restaurants and farm stands are at their best. Our regional food guide has the month-by-month view.

Grand Junction is about a four-hour drive from Denver on I-70 in good conditions, through Glenwood Canyon. That makes it a natural endpoint for the drive described in our road-trip dining guide.

Questions

Common questions

Are there MICHELIN restaurants in Telluride?

Yes, one. The National at 100 E. Colorado Ave. joined the MICHELIN Recommended list in 2026, the first restaurant in the San Juan Mountains to be included in the guide.

Does Grand Junction have any award-winning restaurants?

Bin 707 Foodbar's chef-owner Josh Niernberg was a 2026 James Beard nominee for Outstanding Chef, a national category. No Grand Junction restaurant was listed in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Colorado.

Are there MICHELIN restaurants in Durango?

Not in the 2026 selection. Durango was eligible for the first time in 2026 and none of its restaurants was listed.

When is the Palisade Peach Festival?

It is held each August at Riverbend Park in Palisade. Check the Palisade Chamber of Commerce for the next year's dates, tickets and shuttle information.
