The ground rules for using this guide.

Effective 10 October 2026. These terms apply to your use of bestrestaurantcolorado.com. Using the site means you accept them.

An independent guide

Best Restaurant Colorado is independent. It has no affiliation with the MICHELIN Guide, the James Beard Foundation, the restaurants it lists or any tourism organization, and none of them endorses it. We refer to award names only to report which restaurants hold them.

Check before you go

We verify facts against the sources listed on each page and date our updates, but restaurant details change constantly, and mountain restaurants in particular change hours and close seasonally. We do not publish hours, prices or phone numbers, and we cannot guarantee that any restaurant is open, that a reservation is available or that information on this site is complete. Please confirm with the restaurant before traveling or paying.

Travel information, such as notes on roads, passes, altitude and weather, is general guidance. Always check official sources, including the Colorado Department of Transportation's COtrip service, and use your own judgement in mountain conditions.

Editorial order

Some guides present restaurants in an editorial order with the criteria explained on the page. That order is our judgement, not a rating or ranking from any award body. We do not publish our own ratings or reviews.
