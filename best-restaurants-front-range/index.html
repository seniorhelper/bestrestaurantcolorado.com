Short answer

Outside Denver, Boulder dominates the Front Range with 11 award-holding restaurants, including Frasca Food and Wine, which kept its MICHELIN star in 2026 and was named James Beard Outstanding Restaurant in 2025. The 2026 MICHELIN Guide also reached Aurora, where Annette earned a Bib Gourmand and Dân Dã a Recommended listing, and Colorado Springs, where Four by Brother Luck and The Penrose Room were both listed. Fort Collins, Longmont and Pueblo have no entries.

Boulder  /  5,328 ft  /  10 entries

A town of a hundred thousand with a national restaurant award.

Has a Colorado restaurant ever won a national James Beard award?

Yes. Frasca Food and Wine in Boulder was named Outstanding Restaurant in 2025, one of the James Beard Foundation's top national categories. It was the only Colorado win that year out of fourteen semifinalists. Frasca also holds a MICHELIN star, which it has kept in every Colorado selection from 2023 through 2026.

Boulder's density of recognition is genuinely unusual. 11 entries in a town of roughly a hundred thousand people, against 37 MICHELIN-recognized restaurants for Denver, a city several times its size, is a far higher rate per resident.

Frasca opened in 2004 and has been recognised by critics and James Beard judges for most of the time since. The kitchen focuses tightly on the cuisine of Friuli-Venezia Giulia in northeastern Italy, and the guide singles out both the precision of the pasta and secondi and the restaurant's use of carefully sourced Colorado ingredients. Its service won a James Beard award of its own.

Blackbelly and Bramble & Hare held two of Colorado's four MICHELIN Green Stars in the 2025 selection, a striking concentration for one town. Blackbelly is built around whole-animal butchery and farm sourcing; Bramble & Hare is the restaurant side of Black Cat Farm. Neither holds a star, which is exactly the point of the Green Star being a separate award.

Basta and Cozobi Fonda Fina carry the Bib Gourmands. Basta is part of Kelly Whitaker's Id Est group, the same group behind the two-starred Wolf's Tailor and the one-starred Brutø in Denver. Cozobi is the newest of the two, from chef Johnny Curiel, whose Denver restaurants Alma Fonda Fina and Mezcaleria Alma both hold stars.

That is worth pausing on. Two of the most decorated restaurant groups in Colorado both chose to put a restaurant in Boulder, and in both cases the Boulder restaurant is the more affordable one.

The Recommended tier fills out the rest: Oak at Fourteenth and Stella's Cucina, Santo on Alpine Avenue serving New Mexican, Zoe Ma Ma on Pearl, the Boulder Dushanbe Tea House, hand-carved in Tajikistan, shipped in pieces and reassembled here as a gift from Boulder's sister city, and, new in 2026, Casa Juani at 901 Pearl Street, a Spanish restaurant from Frasca alumni Eduardo Valle Lobo and Kelly Jeun that opened in February 2026.

If you have one evening on the Front Range and you are not tied to Denver, Boulder is the stronger bet on pure hit rate.

The small towns

Small towns, single rooms, national attention.

This is still one of the most interesting patterns on the Front Range. None of these towns was eligible for MICHELIN consideration when their chefs were first recognized, and in 2026 only Aurora made the guide.

Lyons  /  5,374 ft

Marigold

Chef Theo Adley was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Best Chef: Mountain, the category covering Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Utah and Wyoming. Lyons has a population in the low thousands and sits at the mouth of the St. Vrain canyon, twenty minutes north of Boulder.

Adley opened a second restaurant, Heretík, in Denver in 2026. It joined the MICHELIN Recommended list that September, and Adley and Jenessa Reiman won the 2026 MICHELIN Sommelier Award there. Marigold itself was not listed.

Edgewater  /  5,335 ft

Gladys

Dave Grant and Rocky Hunter were first-time James Beard semifinalists in 2026 for Best Chef: Mountain. Edgewater is a small city of a few thousand people wedged between Denver and Lakewood, and it is the kind of place a guide with a city-limits boundary misses entirely.

Parker  /  5,869 ft

Poulette Bakeshop

A 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Outstanding Bakery, run by Alan Ramos and Carolyn Nugent. Parker is a southeastern suburb in Douglas County and the highest-elevation town on this page.

Outstanding Bakery is a national category, not a regional one, which makes this a stronger signal than the town's profile would suggest.

Aurora  /  5,471 ft

Annette

Chef-owner Caroline Glover won Best Chef: Mountain outright in 2022. Annette sits in Stanley Marketplace, and for four years it was the standing example of what the old MICHELIN boundary excluded: a James Beard-winning restaurant in Colorado's third-largest city that inspectors were not permitted to visit.

That changed in 2026. In Aurora's first year of eligibility, Annette earned a Bib Gourmand, and Dân Dã, a Vietnamese restaurant at 9945 E. Colfax Avenue run by sisters An and Thao Nguyen, joined the Recommended list.

The gap

Front Range cities with no entry, even in a statewide year.

Does Colorado Springs have any MICHELIN restaurants?

Yes, since 2026. Four by Brother Luck at 321 N. Tejon St. and The Penrose Room at The Broadmoor both joined the MICHELIN Recommended list in the first statewide selection, and staff at both won 2026 special awards. Before 2026 no Colorado Springs restaurant could be considered, because the city was outside the guide's Colorado boundary.

We are not going to fill this gap by inventing a list. There is no shortage of pages ranking Fort Collins restaurants, and a meaningful share of them are automatically generated, unsourced, or both. Adding another would make this register worse.

What we can say is that all four cities were eligible in 2026 and nothing was listed. Fort Collins is a university city with an established brewery and farm-to-table culture. Longmont is home to Black Cat Farmstead, which Westword named as a restaurant it hoped to see in the expanded selection. Pueblo grows the chile that is Colorado's own.

The guide is revised every year. When any of these cities appears in a MICHELIN or James Beard announcement, the entry goes on this page the same week.

Front Range versus the mountains

Two different businesses wearing the same apron.

What the Front Range has

A year-round customer. Boulder, Fort Collins and Colorado Springs restaurants serve residents fifty-two weeks a year. There is no mud season here, no two-week shutdown, and no month where the town empties out.

Staff who can afford to live nearby. Housing on the Front Range is expensive by national standards but nothing like the resort towns, where workers commute over passes, share rooms or live in vehicles. A Boulder restaurant can hire from a real labour market.

Supply lines that work. Deliveries do not cross a pass that closes. Produce from the San Luis Valley and the Grand Valley reaches Front Range kitchens faster and cheaper than it reaches Aspen.

Milder altitude. At 5,000 feet water boils at about 203 degrees. That is a real adjustment from sea level, but it is a long way from the 194 degrees a Breckenridge kitchen contends with.

What the mountains have

A guest with no default. A visitor in Vail has no usual place and no habitual choice. They search, they read, and they decide. A Boulder resident already knows where they are going on Friday.

Price tolerance. Someone who has paid for a ski week will pay for dinner. That funds ambition a Longmont restaurant cannot easily fund.

Scarcity. Six good rooms in a resort town beats sixty in a city, from the operator's side of the ledger.

Neither model is better. They reward completely different things, which is why comparing a Boulder Bib Gourmand to an Aspen tasting menu tells you almost nothing. Sorting this register by elevation instead of by rank is our attempt to let you see which situation you are actually in.

For the mountains, see mountain town restaurants.

Should I eat in Denver or Boulder if I only have one night?

Denver if you want the most options and the highest ceiling, since it holds the state's only two-star restaurant and seven of the nine one-stars. Boulder if you want the best odds of a very good meal without a hard-to-get reservation, because 11 recognized restaurants in a small walkable town means less competition per table. Boulder also has the only James Beard Outstanding Restaurant winner in Colorado history.

Questions

Eating on the Front Range.

Missing something? Send it to us and we will answer it and add it here.

Denver itself is covered at Best Restaurant Denver. The statewide register has every entry, and the MICHELIN page has the full 2026 list.

What is the best restaurant in Boulder?

Frasca Food and Wine at 1738 Pearl Street, on the strongest available evidence. It has held a MICHELIN star in every Colorado selection since 2023, including 2026, and was named James Beard Outstanding Restaurant in 2025, which is a national award rather than a regional one. Its cooking is focused on Friuli-Venezia Giulia in northeastern Italy, and it is known as much for its service and wine program as for the food.

Are there MICHELIN-starred restaurants outside Denver on the Front Range?

One. Frasca Food and Wine in Boulder. Every other starred restaurant in Colorado is in Denver, apart from Bosq in Aspen. Boulder also had two MICHELIN Green Stars in the 2025 selection, at Blackbelly and Bramble & Hare, and holds two Bib Gourmands at Basta and Cozobi Fonda Fina.

Are there Fort Collins or Colorado Springs restaurants on this list?

Fort Collins was eligible for the first time in the 2026 statewide selection and none of its restaurants was listed. Colorado Springs is now on the list: Four by Brother Luck and The Penrose Room both joined as Recommended restaurants in 2026, and both won special awards.

Which small Front Range towns have award-winning restaurants?

Lyons has Marigold, where chef Theo Adley was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Best Chef: Mountain. Edgewater has Gladys, where Dave Grant and Rocky Hunter were semifinalists in the same category. Parker has Poulette Bakeshop, a 2026 Outstanding Bakery semifinalist. Aurora has Annette, where Caroline Glover won Best Chef: Mountain outright in 2022 and which earned a MICHELIN Bib Gourmand in 2026.

Is Boulder's restaurant scene better than Denver's?

Not larger, but disproportionately strong for its size. Boulder has roughly a seventh of Denver's population and holds 11 award entries against Denver's 37 MICHELIN listings, a far higher rate per resident. Boulder also holds the only James Beard Outstanding Restaurant winner in state history.

What is the Boulder Dushanbe Tea House?

A MICHELIN Recommended restaurant at 1770 13th Street that was hand-carved in Tajikistan and given to Boulder by its sister city, Dushanbe. It was shipped in pieces and reassembled. It is on the guide for its food, but it is also one of the more unusual dining rooms in the United States.

How far in advance should I book on the Front Range?

Less far than in the mountains. Frasca is the exception and should be booked several weeks out, particularly at weekends. Most Boulder Bib Gourmand and Recommended restaurants can be booked within a few days, and several hold bar seats for walk-ins. The small-town restaurants in Lyons, Edgewater and Parker are smaller rooms and benefit from a week's notice.

Does altitude affect Front Range restaurants the way it affects mountain kitchens?

Less, but it still applies. Colorado State University Extension puts the boiling point of water at 203 degrees Fahrenheit at 5,000 feet against 212 at sea level, so Denver and Boulder kitchens still work with liquids that never get as hot as a sea-level recipe assumes. The effect is real but far milder than at 9,600 feet in Breckenridge, and Front Range bakers generally need smaller adjustments than mountain bakers do.

What is the difference between this page and the Denver restaurant coverage?

This page covers the Front Range outside Denver: Boulder, Lyons, Edgewater, Aurora, Parker and the cities still awaiting a first entry. Denver metro has its own site at Best Restaurant Denver, which goes neighbourhood by neighbourhood through the city itself. The statewide register on our home page contains everything, Denver included.

Can a restaurant buy a place on this page?

No. Register entries come only from the MICHELIN Guide and the James Beard Foundation, and no restaurant can pay to be added or moved. The site is supported by display advertising served by Google, which is kept separate from the content.
