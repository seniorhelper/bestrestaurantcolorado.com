How many people are looking

Colorado drew 95.4 million visitors in 2024, up 2.3%, mostly on the strength of day trips, according to the state’s visitor research reported by CPR News. The Governor’s office put total visitor spending at $28.5 billion.

In mountain towns, a large share of the dinner crowd is from out of town, and they rarely know your name before they search. Showing up with a photo, hours and a readable menu matters more here than almost anywhere.

Questions owners ask

Is a basic listing really free?

Yes. Restaurants in Colorado can request an accurate basic listing on Best Restaurant Colorado for free: name, area, cuisine, price range, hours and links. We verify you’re the owner or manager before changes go live. Featured and Menu Pro are optional upgrades, and nothing you buy changes whether we keep you listed.

What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

Featured ($29 a month) gives you a top spot on Best Restaurant Colorado with a badge, a photo, a pitch and your link. Menu Pro ($49 a month or $490 a year) includes the same Featured placement plus a hosted, Google-readable menu page, a full menu update every quarter and NFC table tags, all done for you.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. We promise the work: a clearly labeled top placement, your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines can read and that we keep current. How many diners choose you still depends on your food, reviews and service.

How fast does it go live?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your photo, pitch and link. Menu Pro menu pages go live within 48 hours of receiving your menu. We confirm you run the restaurant first, so a quick reply to our welcome email keeps things moving.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime online and stay live until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Menu Pro yearly is one payment of $490 for twelve months, which is two months free compared with paying monthly.

My restaurant is in a small mountain town. Is there a page for it?

Probably. The guide covers the Front Range, Colorado Springs, Aspen, Vail and Breckenridge, Telluride and the Western Slope, plus road-trip routes. If your town isn’t covered yet, request a free listing and tell us where you are; Featured spots are placed on the regional page closest to you.
