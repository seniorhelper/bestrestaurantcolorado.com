Privacy policy

What we collect, how Google advertising cookies work, and how to opt out.

Effective 10 October 2026. Best Restaurant Colorado (bestrestaurantcolorado.com) comes from Eye To Ad Media, a Denver publisher. This page describes the limited information the site handles and the advertising technology it uses.

Messages you send

Our contact form asks for your name, your email address, a topic and a message. FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) relays the form to our email inbox. We keep messages only as long as we need them to respond or to make a correction, we never sell or share them for marketing, and we do not sign anyone up for newsletters.

Technical data

Each page load leaves a routine entry in our host's logs, which automatically record standard request details such as your IP address, browser and device type, the address of the page and the time. Those logs help keep the site secure and working.

Google AdSense, cookies and your choices

Ads on this site come from Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors place cookies that let them choose ads according to a visitor's earlier visits to this site and to other websites. Through those advertising cookies, Google and its partners can tailor the ads you see here to your browsing across the web.

To switch off ad personalization from Google, visit Google Ads Settings. Many other ad networks honour opt-outs registered at www.aboutads.info. For more on how Google handles data from sites that show its ads, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, Google will show a consent message before using cookies or similar technology for personalized advertising. You can accept, decline or change your choice later. If you decline, you may still see ads that are not personalized, which can still use cookies for limited purposes such as fraud prevention and counting how often an ad is shown.

You can also block or clear cookies in your browser. The site's content works without them.

Other services the pages load

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google. We do not use social media pixels or other tracking scripts beyond Google's advertising services.

External links

Links in our guides lead to restaurants, award organizations, public agencies and newsrooms. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children's privacy

This guide is written for grown-ups planning meals and travel, and it is not designed to gather data from anyone under 13.

Questions and requests

To ask that we delete a message you sent, or to ask about this policy, write through the contact page or to info@eyetoad.com. We will update the effective date above if this policy changes.
